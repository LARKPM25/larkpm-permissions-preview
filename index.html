Two ways to allocate access

Both options keep named roles with a base set plus add-ons organized by module. They differ in what an add-on is, and how wide the Administrator base stays. Hub write-up: C-LarkPM docs/permissions-evaluation-2026-08-26.md ([PERM-EVAL-1]).

Proposal 1

Option A — Slim role + module packs

Administrator is a small office floor. Marketing, Financial, Production, and the rest are named packs you turn on. Stops “make them Admin” from unlocking Marketing.
